## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Core concept: City-as-Repo (the city as an open-source repository).** The Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt should not merely be "a city designed by AI" but "a city continuously operated by AI". The Luoduo Robot Brain (SkyDuo orchestrator), as the scheduling hub (Orchestration Layer) of the urban operating system, decomposes planning, operation, and service into orchestratable, reviewable, and auditable agent tasks — which aligns naturally with the charter spirit of "AI-native innovation, no label-only AI" `[charter.4]` `[agent.1]`.

**Five functions implemented.** Corresponding to the taskbook's five functions `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`: ① AI full-stack autonomous innovation system; ② world-class AI innovation ecosystem; ③ AI+ scenario empowerment new paradigm; ④ intelligent AI-vibrant city; ⑤ global discourse power in AI governance. Luoduo maps the five functions onto runnable urban-operating-system modules with an "agent-first" principle.

**Regional synergy (review dimension `regional_synergy`).** The belt is rooted in Haidian and links the Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Technological Development Area, and the Jing-Jin-Ji innovation synergy `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`; the Zhongguancun science-service wing undertakes global factor allocation and capital empowerment, while the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing undertakes AI scenario landing and vitality cultivation `[source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]`.

**Global reference cases (public-source review, conceptual inspiration, not factual claims).** To support the AI innovation ecosystem map, six representative practices reported publicly are compiled as methodological references (specific data must be cross-verified against official/first-hand sources; this proposal draws no conclusions from them) `[source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]`:
1. Toronto Quayside / Sidewalk Labs — lessons on data governance and privacy design for waterfront smart districts (project terminated; value lies in methodological reflection);
2. Singapore Punggol Digital District — government-led digital twin and smart-park operation;
3. Amsterdam AI / data trust — safeguarding public value through data trusts;
4. Helsinki AI testbed — city-scale AI ethics sandbox;
5. Barcelona urban data platform — open-data-driven public services;
6. Boston Kendall Square — high-density mixed innovation-city district.
These cases serve only as reference coordinates for the ecosystem map, reflecting review dimensions such as `planning_innovation`, `industry_support`, and `long_term_operation_value` `[dimension:planning_innovation]` `[dimension:industry_support]` `[dimension:long_term_operation_value]`.

**Ecosystem map.** The AI innovation ecosystem map (`ecosystem_map`) depicts four rings — factors, actors, scenarios, governance — covering eight factor mechanisms: land/space/industry/capital/talent/compute/data/scenarios `[agent.2]`. Land-use structure below.
